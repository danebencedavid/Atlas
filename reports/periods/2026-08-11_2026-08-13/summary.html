Erratum, issued 2026-08-19. This edition was published with incomplete station observations. It achieved 300/432 ten-minute records (69%) across its window, and its final day, 2026-08-13, carried only 12/144 records (8%). The build ran before the provider regenerated its daily export, so the last 22 hours of the window were unobserved at the time of writing. Quantities derived from the station record over the whole window are affected, in particular:
  • objective frontal passage detection, which reads the station series directly
  • the objective phenomena ledger, which draws on station, radar and lightning
  • the period mean temperature, precipitation total and maximum gust in the station observation ledger
Gridded quantities are unaffected: the hourly analysis was complete. This edition is annotated rather than regenerated, so it still shows what was published on the day.
